# Guild membership access

Updated 2026-11-18: the toolkit's entry requirement is native membership in **Tranquility**, regardless of guild rank. There is no vault, riddle, shared PIN, Discord code, character unlock record or rank-promotion requirement for opening the addon.

Characters outside Tranquility, and characters whose guild identity is not yet available, cannot enter. They see a closable message asking them to join the guild, with the immediately visible copyable invite **https://discord.gg/CMjbSRYt6**. Guild membership changes are rechecked through the existing native events; leaving hides toolkit windows.

Legacy vault records do not grant or deny entry. Legacy gateway packets are ignored and cannot prompt, unlock or automatically promote anyone. Optional officer-managed Discord identity/rank linking remains separate from addon admission.

Officer administration, bank permissions, job eligibility, payout policies and existing work-board restrictions are unchanged. Admitting every guild member does not grant administrative or financial authority.

Regression checks cover new members, recruits, missing rank data, outsiders, unavailable/restricted guild names, joining/leaving, dismissal, old packets and separate officer privileges. No guild messages, promotions or Discord messages were sent during this change.
