## Short reviewer note

Tranquility is a modular guild toolkit with manual recruitment preparation, local research/journaling, deliberate summon clicks and a guild-only activity board. The Dungeon module includes documented, rate-limited automatic XP warnings that can be disabled per session. Optional Uber is bundled, not required by core, and has separate native-mail pricing/reporting behavior documented in its README. No executable companion, account data, credentials or combat automation is in the distribution. Source is readable Lua under MIT.
