# Tranquility Guild Toolkit

Our guild tools in one place: private dungeon/PvP group planning, loot notes, auction research, manual recruiting and party helpers.

**Current candidate: 1.0.0-beta.4 for WoW: Forever, Interface 16001.** We are preparing the listing while waiting for Uber's separate submission result. This is not a claim that either project has been approved.

## Install and open

Extract the ZIP's 13 addon folders directly into Interface/AddOns, not inside another toolkit folder. Enable Tranquility plus the feature modules you want. TranquilityLFG and Uber are separate optional companions. Restart after a fresh install, then open /tranq or /tq, or click the TQ minimap button.

Keep Tranquility enabled for its ten modules. Disable any module you do not need in WoW's AddOns menu. Help and settings explains the controls and local data options.

## Find guild groups without repeated chat advertisements

Open Guild LFG or type /tqlfg. Tranquility guild members with the addon can list dungeons, battleground/world-PvP activities, arena plans or rated-battleground plans. Select up to five activities, your role, group size and a short note. Browse matching guildmates and click to invite through normal game permissions.

Listings travel through the guild addon channel, expire if not refreshed and start unqueued after reload. This board coordinates plans; it does not enter Blizzard matchmaking or enable a mode unavailable on the client. Leave your listing when your plans change.

## Recruit deliberately

Run a native WHO search yourself, import results and review players with confirmed empty guild fields. Prepare a personalized greeting or invite command for one person, then copy/send it yourself. Mark contacted or ignored names to skip them later. Unknown guild data is not treated as guildless. Respect declines and requests not to be contacted.

## Keep useful loot notes

The Loot journal remembers observed dungeon/open-world items and available sources. Seen, roll-offered and received entries are different. Unknown sources remain unknown; an entry is not a guaranteed drop table or proof that you personally received the item.

## Research auction prices

Record complete results from searches you perform, or enter observed unit prices. Compare against daily-low history after at least three prior sample days. Ignore unwanted items and consult source notes. Asking prices are research, not proof of demand or profit. No automatic auction transactions or repeated queries are performed.

## Dungeon information and XP warnings

View available tank HP, healer mana and partial mob observations. Keep the assessment local or click the rate-limited party-summary button.

The Dungeon module also starts with automatic, rate-limited XP warnings enabled. It compares available party levels with observed enemies and warns about the implemented level-gap rule. /tranqxp off disables warnings for the session; /tranqxp on enables them. This is separate from clicked summaries, and it predicts observed kill-XP conditions rather than measuring actual XP or every enemy in an instance.

## Prepare party summons

The basic Summons checklist skips players already reported in your zone. Each ritual needs your physical click, spell, reagents and helpers. It does not perform an unattended sequence or certify delivery from an attempted cast.

Optional Uber opens its separate summons/Mage portal service: provider cards, prices, hours, advance requests, setup tracking and receipt/delivery timelines. Uber has its own payment and data-sharing rules. It is not required for the core or basic summons checklist. Its separate approval is pending; read its included guide before using paid services.

## Other tools

Regions prepares manual WHO queries and stores returned-row samples, not a server census. Gear shows available target equipment and marks incomplete inspections; it does not rate wealth or skill. Phases compares channel observations as hints rather than verified world-layer IDs. The optional DB supplies Classic reference notes marked unverified for Forever.

## Data and support

Persistent toolkit data uses local SavedVariables. LFG shares listings with participating guildmates; clicked reports and enabled XP warnings use party chat. Optional Uber exchanges its own service records with participating clients and creator. Do not include sensitive information in notes or public bug reports.

For issues, include the addon version, exact game build, enabled modules, reproduction steps and full Lua error. Remove private names/account details before posting to the published support channel. No external account, desktop app or third-party library is required.

Free readable Lua under MIT. No paid software unlock, automated combat, movement or loot collection. Independent community project, not endorsed by Blizzard or CurseForge. Gallery images should show actual gameplay; branding/design previews are separate.

## New group finder and Discord lounge

Dedicated Dungeon, Battleground and Arena pages now include casual/ranked categories, bracket ratings where available, a passive member directory and green leader flags. Leaders can preview and arm a role/level/loot-aware group builder, with optional diversity and leader-only carry settings. Available is different from READY; only ready matches are auto-invited. Invitations are paced and remain subject to native permissions.

The Discord lounge mirrors explicit public-channel snapshots through a trusted officer and the existing optional bot. Members can queue replies without Discord; they stay pending until an actual bot-post acknowledgement is imported. This is not a direct live Discord connection. Owner credentials/intents still need local setup; nothing has been posted automatically.
