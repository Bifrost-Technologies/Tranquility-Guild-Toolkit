## One-paragraph introduction

Tranquility Guild Toolkit brings your everyday guild tools into one menu: a private guild LFG board, manual recruitment preparation, loot and item-source notes, observed auction-price history, dungeon information and a physical-click party summons checklist. Enable the modules you want and open /tranq or /tq. The bundle also includes optional Uber integration for its separate summons and Mage portal service. Targets World of Warcraft: Forever, Interface 16001.
